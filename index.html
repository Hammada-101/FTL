Method. 10-day CHIRPS rainfall for 61 districts is added up from 1 November and compared with the normal amount by the same date (1991/92–2020/21). Governorates and Syria are built from districts, weighted by area. The alert lines were learned from 45 seasons and four documented droughts: below % is RED, up to % AMBER, above that GREEN. Crop greenness (MODIS NDVI) uses prototype lines of 80% and 90%. Against FAO's national barley yields for 1982–2024, every RED outside the war years came before a bad barley year; district alerts are not yet validated because district yields are not published.

    
Data. WFP Rainfall and NDVI Indicators (CHIRPS v2, MODIS) on HDX · FAOSTAT · NASA POWER · World Bank Climate Change Knowledge Portal (CMIP6) · OCHA administrative boundaries. Context: SANA, FAO, FAO GIEWS, GEOGLAM Crop Monitor.
